It started in February as fourteen utilities in a SPA, so I could stop pasting tokens into sites I did not know.

In September I rebuilt it in Astro and Svelte and it grew to 52: the ones I open every day, plus the ones you need to work in Spain, like DNI, IBAN, VAT or IRPF withholding, which I never found in one place.
